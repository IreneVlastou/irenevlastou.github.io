Irene Vlastou

I'm a classicist and papyrologist based in Athens, currently doing a Master's in Digital Methods for the Humanities at the Department of Informatics, Athens University of Economics and Business. I work on the point where ancient written material meets computational method: palaeography, papyrus editing, knowledge graphs and text annotation.

I hold a Master's in Papyrology and Classics from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens, where my thesis on palaeographical terminology and non-literary writing in papyri received the Lysimachos Kaftantzoglou Prize. Since July 2026 I have been a junior research associate at the Laboratory for Political and Institutional Theory and the History of Ideas at NKUA. Since 2024 I have also been an annotator for the Greek corpus of PARSEME at the University of Oxford, and I recently worked with the Digital Assets Department of the British School at Athens on GIS material for the Fitch Laboratory.

I'm open to research positions and collaborations in digital classics, papyrology and cultural heritage data. Do get in touch.

Research

I work on Greek papyri and palaeography, on the vocabulary we use to describe ancient hands, and on how digital methods — corpora, annotation, ontologies and spatial data — can be applied to material of this kind.
